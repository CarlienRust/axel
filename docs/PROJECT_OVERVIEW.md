**The person we're building for**

An adult in South Africa — likely 25 to 45 — who has spent years feeling like they're operating on a different frequency to everyone around them. Intelligent, capable, full of ideas and ambition. But chronically overwhelmed. Paralysed at the exact moment they want to move. Exhausted by the gap between who they know they are and what they're actually able to produce on any given day. Undiagnosed, or recently diagnosed but without real support. They haven't failed because they lack ability. They've struggled because nobody gave them the right conditions — or the right tools.

---

**The moment Axel exists for**

They sit down to work. They want to move. And instead of moving, everything arrives at once — the emails, the decisions, the unfinished things, the big goals, the small tasks, the fear of starting the wrong thing, the fear of starting at all. The noise gets louder. Nothing gets done. The day slips. And they go to bed feeling like they failed again.

That moment is what Axel is built for.

---

**What we believe**

ADHD is not a ceiling. It is a different operating system running in an environment designed for a different one. The noise is real. The paralysis is neurological, not moral. The gap between potential and output is not a character flaw — it is the result of having no tools built for the way this brain actually works.

This person is enough. They always were.

---

**What Axel does — and only this**

One. The noise dump. Open Axel when everything is loud. Pour it all in — voice, text, whatever. Axel listens, sorts, and hands back one thing. Not a list. The one most important thing right now. Everything else is held safely.

Two. The reframe. Short, honest, plain-language content that shifts how they see themselves. Not motivational. Not clinical. Grounded in real research about how the ADHD brain works and what it's genuinely capable of.

Three. The preparation tool. For when they're ready to seek a formal diagnosis or professional support. Builds a picture of their own experience over time — in their words, at their pace — so they walk into any appointment already knowing what to say.

Four. The landing tools. For moments of dysregulation, overwhelm, or emotional flood. Short, practical, interruptible. For the exact moment the spiral starts.

---

**What Axel never does**

Diagnoses. Judges. Tracks streaks. Sends guilt notifications. Makes them feel behind. Sells their data. Locks the most important features behind a paywall.

---

**What success looks like at thirty days**

The person using Axel can say — genuinely — "I am not broken. I am different. And I am capable of everything I came here to do."

That shift is the product. Everything else is the mechanism.

Free for the core experience, always. Small monthly subscription for unlimited AI interactions and the preparation tool. Never monetised through data or advertising.
